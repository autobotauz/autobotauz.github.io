What it does

When you type /chatlog in Dark Age of Camelot, the game saves everything in your chat window to a file called chat.log. Chat Gator reads that file and shows you what the game doesn't: how much damage and healing you did and took, which spells and styles landed, crit or were resisted, what your pets did, how often you blocked, parried and evaded, and where your realm points came from.

Your session is split into fights automatically, and you can zoom any chart to a single moment. In Chrome or Edge, Chat Gator can follow your log while you play, so the numbers and the cooldown tracker keep up as you go. It's built and tested on real logs from the Eden server.

Bask

A live group meter for up to eight players. Each player's Chat Gator shares only combat numbers, so the group sees damage, healing, resists, interrupts and cooldowns side by side. No chat is shared, and a bask is deleted after 12 idle hours.

Your log stays private

Chat Gator works entirely in your browser. Your chat.log is never uploaded; the site only sends a small note when you start a session so it can count how many sessions people start. The privacy policy has the details, including how ads and cookies work.
